# Competitor research — driver trips scope

## Scope and evidence

This update covers the Driver Upcoming Trips feature requested for VoltStop. It is a product/codebase review, not a fresh market study. No competitor websites, current Google Play listings, or vendor documentation were independently verified for this feature update, so this document makes no comparative feature, pricing, or market-share claims.

## VoltStop baseline observed

- The driver app already plans NH-16 routes between the configured Srikakulam–Vijayawada corridor stops, uses a saved EV, estimates route energy, and can show VoltStop vendor pitstops.
- Its Trips navigation previously focused on charger bookings and hotel stays; there was no driver trip lifecycle or driver trip discovery API.
- VoltStop has no passenger trip booking, seat reservation, fare, insurance, or trip payment implementation in this scope.

## Product implication

Driver trip scheduling and optional browse-only discovery fit the authenticated mobile driver app. Driver trip controls do not belong in the vendor portal, whose existing users manage vendor operations. A future competitor comparison should verify, from current official product pages and store listings, how comparable EV corridor or car-pooling products handle trip listings, booking, privacy, cancellation, and safety before making roadmap claims.
